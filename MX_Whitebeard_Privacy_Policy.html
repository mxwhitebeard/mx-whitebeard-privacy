This Privacy Policy explains how 白胡子文化传媒有限公司 (“we”, “our”, or “the Company”) collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information processed through MX Whitebeard Commerce, including information obtained from authorized TikTok Shop accounts.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features authorized by a merchant, MX Whitebeard Commerce may process:

  • Authorized shop information, such as shop identifiers and shop status.
  • Product information, such as product IDs, titles, images, SKU identifiers, and product status.
  • Order and sales-performance information needed for internal business analysis, such as order ID, product ID, SKU ID, quantity, order status, transaction amount, and order time.
  • Application authorization information required to maintain a merchant's authorized TikTok Shop connection.
  • Internal sourcing records, including source-product links, source search keywords, collection tasks, source sales data, source ratings, and collection dates.

MX Whitebeard Commerce is designed to avoid persistently storing buyer personal information that is not required for our legitimate business purposes. Buyer telephone numbers, detailed delivery addresses, and other unnecessary buyer personal information are not intended to be retained for sourcing or product-performance analytics.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate business and system-operation purposes, including:

  • Connecting and identifying authorized TikTok Shop stores.
  • Synchronizing shop and product information.
  • Analyzing product and sales performance.
  • Linking store performance to historical sourcing records and source search keywords.
  • Managing internal users, operators, stores, sourcing tasks, and product records.
  • Maintaining system security, troubleshooting, auditing, and service reliability.

3. Legal and Authorized Access

We access TikTok Shop data only after the relevant merchant authorizes the application through TikTok Shop's official authorization process. We do not intentionally access a merchant's TikTok Shop data after authorization has been revoked or the shop has been disconnected.

4. Data Minimization

We follow data-minimization and purpose-limitation principles. We seek to process only the information reasonably necessary for store management, product synchronization, sourcing analysis, and sales-performance analysis.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, including access controls, password-protected accounts, endpoint security, firewall protection, automatic screen locking, restricted administrator access, and centralized data management.

Production TikTok Shop API communications use secure HTTPS/TLS connections. Sensitive application credentials such as App Secrets and authorization tokens are restricted to authorized systems and personnel and are not intended to be exposed in normal employee interfaces.

6. Access Control

Access to MX Whitebeard Commerce is limited according to job responsibilities and the principle of least privilege. Employees are granted access only to the operators, stores, tasks, and business information required for their duties. Administrative privileges are restricted to authorized personnel.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell TikTok Shop protected data. We do not disclose protected data to unrelated third parties except where necessary for an authorized business function, required by law, required by applicable platform rules, or necessary to use an approved technical service provider.

8. Data Retention

Protected data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for authorized business purposes, legal obligations, security, and auditing. Historical sourcing records that do not contain unnecessary buyer personal information may be retained for legitimate internal product-selection and business analytics.

9. Data Deletion and Correction Requests

Where applicable, merchants or authorized users may request access, correction, updating, or deletion of information associated with their use of MX Whitebeard Commerce. We will review and process valid requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

When a TikTok Shop authorization is revoked or a business relationship ends, we stop using the associated authorization and will delete or render unusable related access credentials. Protected customer data that is no longer required will be securely deleted, subject to applicable legal or regulatory retention obligations.

10. Security Incidents

We maintain internal security-incident procedures for identifying, assessing, containing, investigating, documenting, and remediating suspected security incidents. Where required, relevant parties will be notified in accordance with applicable legal, contractual, or platform requirements.

11. International and Regional Processing

Our current internal processing environment is based in China. If our infrastructure or data-processing locations materially change, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement appropriate security and compliance controls.

12. Children's Data

MX Whitebeard Commerce is a business tool intended for authorized merchants and company personnel. It is not intended to collect personal data directly from children.

13. Policy Updates

We review this Privacy Policy periodically and may update it when there are significant changes to our systems, services, legal obligations, security requirements, or data-processing practices. The current version will be published at this page.

本隐私政策说明白胡子文化传媒有限公司如何通过 MX Whitebeard Commerce 收集、使用、存储、保护及删除相关信息，包括经商家授权后从 TikTok Shop 获取的数据。

1. 我们处理的信息

根据商家授权的功能，我们可能处理：授权店铺信息、商品信息、订单及销售表现信息、应用授权信息，以及内部采品历史记录（如原商品链接、搜索关键词、采集任务、采集时销量、评分和日期等）。

系统的设计目标是不为采品或商品表现分析长期保存与业务目的无关的买家个人信息，例如买家电话号码、详细收货地址等。

2. 信息用途

我们仅将信息用于授权店铺连接、商品同步、销售表现分析、采品来源追溯、内部运营管理、系统安全、排错和审计等合法业务用途。

3. 授权访问

只有在商家通过 TikTok Shop 官方授权流程授权后，我们才访问相关店铺数据。授权撤销或店铺解绑后，我们不会继续使用该授权访问店铺数据。

4. 数据最小化

我们遵循数据最小化和目的限定原则，仅处理实现店铺管理、商品同步、采品分析及销售表现分析所合理需要的信息。

5. 数据安全

我们采用访问控制、密码保护账号、终端安全、防火墙、自动锁屏、管理员权限限制及集中式数据管理等措施保护数据。正式生产环境中的 TikTok Shop API 通信使用 HTTPS/TLS。

6. 访问控制

员工仅可访问与其岗位职责有关的运营人、店铺、任务和业务数据。管理员权限仅授予经授权人员。

7. 数据共享

我们不会出售 TikTok Shop 受保护数据。除合法业务需要、法律要求、平台规则要求或使用经批准的技术服务提供商外，不会向无关第三方披露受保护数据。

8. 数据保留与删除

受保护数据仅在实现合法业务目的、法律义务、安全和审计所需的合理期限内保留。店铺解绑或授权撤销后，我们停止使用对应授权并删除或使相关访问凭据失效。不再需要的受保护客户数据会在适用法律或监管要求允许的情况下安全删除。

9. 数据访问、更正和删除请求

在适用情况下，商家或授权用户可以提出数据访问、更正、更新或删除请求。我们会根据适用要求审核并处理有效请求。

10. 安全事件

我们维护内部安全事件处理流程，用于识别、评估、控制、调查、记录和整改安全事件，并在法律、合同或平台规则要求时通知相关方。
